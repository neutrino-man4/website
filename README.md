## Contents

### `bloch/`
Interactive visualization of qubits on the Bloch Sphere. Renders quantum states as arrows on the unit sphere with state manipulation in addition to the possibility of viewing the effect of various quantum gates.

### `plots/`
Aesthetic directory listing scripts if you want to share plots with your collaborators. Take a look at [etpwww.etp.kit.edu/~abal/plots](https://etpwww.etp.kit.edu/~abal/plots).

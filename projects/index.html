Project pages for selected research works, with interactive figures, code, and paper links.

1P1Q

A lossless quantum encoding scheme assigning each jet constituent to a single qubit via spherical Bloch-sphere coordinates. Demonstrated via a quantum autoencoder for anomaly detection and a variational quantum circuit for top-quark jet tagging, validated on real CMS experimental data.

Quantum ML Jet Physics Anomaly Detection

QUIVER

Enriches classical ML models with a quantum Fisher information view derived from variational quantum circuits, yielding richer feature representations. Demonstrates improved jet tagging at the LHC and molecular property prediction on the QM9 dataset.

Quantum Fisher Jet Tagging Molecular ML
